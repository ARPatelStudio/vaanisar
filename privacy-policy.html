1. Data Collection & Local Storage

VaaniSar does not require account registration and does not collect, sell, rent, or upload your personal information to Amit Patel or ARK PATEL STUDIO servers.

All content you open or import—including PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT, HTML, Markdown, CSV, JSON, Images, Web Articles, WhatsApp shared texts/files, and Camera Scans—along with your bookmarks, favorites, appearance settings, voice preferences, and reading progress are stored strictly locally inside your device's private application storage (filesDir and SharedPreferences).

2. On-Device OCR, Offline Voice & Audio Export

  • On-Device Text Recognition (OCR): Hindi (Devanagari) and English text extraction from PDF pages, gallery images, and scanned pages is performed locally on your device using on-device Google ML Kit models.
  • Camera Scan-to-Voice: When you tap Scan Page, VaaniSar delegates photo capture to your device's built-in camera app via secure FileProvider temporary URIs. Captured temporary image files are automatically deleted immediately after OCR text extraction completes.
  • Offline Voice Narration & WAV Export: Document reading, Sleep Timer, lock-screen controls, and VaaniCast dual-voice playback use Android's installed Text-to-Speech engine. Offline .wav audio files are generated locally and saved to your device's Music/VaaniSar folder (via Android MediaStore) or shared with other apps only when you explicitly tap Save to Music or Share Audio.

3. Optional AI Features, Voice Input & Web Articles

  • Optional Gemini AI Tools: If you choose to use AI Summary, Saral (ELI5), Ask Q&A, VaaniCast Podcast, or Study Mode (Quiz & Flashcards), you supply your own Google AI Studio Gemini API key. Your API key is stored locally on your device in private app preferences and transmitted securely via HTTPS request headers (x-goog-api-key) directly to Google's Gemini API together with only the active document or page text and your prompt.
  • Optional Web Article Reader: When you paste or share an http:// or https:// link, VaaniSar connects directly to that website solely to download and extract clean readable article text for your offline library.
  • Optional Hands-Free Voice Questions: When you tap the microphone button in the Ask Q&A tab, VaaniSar launches Android's system speech recognition dialog (RecognizerIntent). VaaniSar does not record or store raw microphone audio in the background.

5. Third-Party Services & Libraries

VaaniSar does not embed third-party behavioral tracking, profiling, or data-broker SDKs. The app relies on the following platform and optional services:

  • Google ML Kit (On-Device Text Recognition v2 Devanagari): Processes image and PDF page text locally on your device.
  • Google Gemini API (Optional, User-Initiated): Used only when you enter your own API key and trigger an AI action. Data sent to Google's API is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API Additional Terms of Service .
  • Open-Source Local Libraries: Jetpack Compose, AndroidX, Kotlin Coroutines, and Apache POI (DOCX parsing) run entirely offline inside the application.

6. User Control, Data Retention & Deletion

Because all VaaniSar data lives locally on your device, you retain complete control at all times:

  • Delete Documents & Bookmarks: Remove any document from your Library to immediately delete its saved internal copy and associated bookmarks, or clear bookmarks inside any reader screen.
  • Clear Temporary Cache: Use the in-app Storage & Cache manager on the Library screen to delete exported WAV cache files, temporary scan leftovers, orphaned files, and in-memory AI caches with one tap.
  • Remove API Keys: Open the Key icon inside the VaaniSar AI sheet and tap Clear to erase your saved Gemini API key from device storage.
  • Complete Removal: Uninstalling VaaniSar permanently removes all private application files, preferences, and bookmarks from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

VaaniSar is a general-audience document reader and study productivity application. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and Amit Patel / ARK PATEL STUDIO does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us via support email, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
